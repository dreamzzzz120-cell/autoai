# AutoAI Monetization

## Positioning

AutoAI should be sold as a vehicle intelligence layer, not as another generic OBD code reader.

Core promise:

> Connect a compatible vehicle interface and let AutoAI continuously interpret the vehicle's observable data, remember its history, detect meaningful changes, and explain what should be investigated next.

## Why customers can pay

The market already supports paid consumer OBD products. FIXD currently lists Premium at $12.99/month or $99.99/year, and sells its hardware separately. Carly monetizes deeper diagnostics and Smart Mechanic features through paid packages. These are evidence that consumers will pay for vehicle-health software when it saves time, reduces uncertainty, or helps avoid unnecessary repair expense.

AutoAI's differentiated paid value should therefore be:

1. Continuous monitoring instead of one-off scanning.
2. Trend interpretation instead of raw sensor numbers.
3. Evidence-backed explanation instead of generic code definitions.
4. Vehicle memory and history instead of starting over at every scan.
5. Repair decision support instead of simply listing a DTC.
6. Explicit uncertainty so users can tell observed facts from AI inference.

## Recommended initial pricing

### Free — $0

Use the product and prove the workflow.

- 1 diagnostic workspace
- Manual symptom/DTC analysis
- Limited telemetry sessions
- Basic evidence-first guidance

### AutoAI Plus — $9.99/month

The main consumer subscription.

- Continuous Bluetooth OBD-II monitoring
- Live vehicle-health dashboard
- DTC and abnormal-trend alerts
- AI interpretation of telemetry changes
- Vehicle diagnostic timeline/history
- Recommended next tests

### AutoAI Pro — $99/year

Annual value plan for owners who want the product permanently connected to the vehicle lifecycle.

- Everything in Plus
- Unlimited monitoring sessions
- Deeper diagnostic evidence reports
- Maintenance forecasting
- Pre-repair decision reports
- Priority diagnostic analysis

## Paid outcome

The product should repeatedly answer five customer questions better than a traditional scanner:

- Is something changing?
- Does it matter?
- What evidence supports that conclusion?
- What should I test next?
- Can I safely keep driving while I investigate?

## Revenue expansion

After the consumer loop is proven, add:

- Pro mechanic workspace: $49–$99/location/month
- Shop/team plans: usage-based or per-bay pricing
- White-label vehicle health reports
- Fleet monitoring
- Hardware bundles
- Paid diagnostic evidence reports

## Do not monetize yet

Do not charge for features that are still unverified on real physical vehicles. The first paid gate should follow successful real-world Bluetooth OBD-II validation.

## Product rule

Never manufacture a vehicle capability to make the paid product look stronger. If a vehicle/interface does not expose a signal, AutoAI must say so.
